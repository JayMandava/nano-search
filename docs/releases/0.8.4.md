# Nano Search 0.8.4

The RM-inspired Carbon face now eases its two upper spars into the inner blue
bezel with matching curved shoulders. The lower feet use the same restrained
flare. The separate six-o'clock collar, open centre-to-six gap, screw-free front
bridge, four matched rear plates, removed side jewels and engraving cleanup remain.
Case, counter interiors, date geometry, main hands and chronograph behavior are
preserved.

The bottom-right indicator now shows phone battery level with L/M/H labels:
0–20% Low, 21–70% Medium and 71–100% High, inclusive. The pointer is absent for
unknown, invalid or absent-battery readings. Battery data comes from Android's
sticky battery broadcast and subsequent battery-change events, without additional
permissions or battery polling in drawing frames. Home/preview Views and wallpaper
hosts stop observation when hidden, detached, screen-off or destroyed and refresh
on return. Accessibility descriptions include battery level when known.

The candidate has 195 JVM tests per debug/release variant, including two opt-in
render-export tests that skip during ordinary runs. Validation uses full suites,
Compose/Robolectric tests, native
Canvas geometry and exact protected-pixel regressions, battery boundary/update and
View/wallpaper lifecycle tests, explicit lint, unsigned R8 assembly, instrumentation
source compilation, release safeguard tests and unsigned APK provenance/alignment
inspection. Current and before renders are actual Robolectric/API35/native-Skia
outputs at representative sizes, with light/dark surrounding backgrounds and lume.
They are not Android device screenshots or phone-performance measurements.

Original Library reference images were located but their supported materialization
failed with a network error; their pixels were not inspected for this revision.
The visual change was assessed against actual v0.8.3 and revised renderer pixels.
No new device/emulator instrumentation, physical TalkBack/OEM-host checks or
phone frame-time profiling was performed. Owner visual/device review remains useful.
Private fonts, personal overlays, production certificate pin, independent v2
verification and both protected release approvals are unchanged.

Version code: 19. Package: `ai.nanosearch.launcher`.
