# Nano Search 0.8.4

The RM-inspired Carbon face has eased upper and lower bridge joins while preserving
the separate six-o'clock collar, open centre-to-six gap, screw-free front bridge,
four matched rear plates, removed side jewels and cleared engraving. The case now
has flatter top/bottom shoulders and a restrained side waist. Its pushers are dark
angled blocks with narrow satin shoulders; the left orange pusher has a fitted
edge. Existing fasteners and touch targets are unchanged.

The bottom-right phone battery indicator uses one curved L/M/H scale and pivot.
Low is 0–20%, Medium 21–70% and High 71–100%, inclusive. Colored arc segments and
small end ticks replace scattered marks; label tilt is limited for readability.
The compact white pointer has a dark pivot and a small orange cue near its head.
Unknown/invalid/absent-battery readings hide the pointer. Android's sticky battery
broadcast and subsequent events provide readings without extra permissions or
per-frame polling. Home Views and wallpaper engines observe only while active and
refresh on return; accessibility descriptions include known battery level.

The final case and scale were reviewed against the actual close-up by a separate
coordinating executor, which supplied measured geometry and independently reviewed
two sets of actual native render comparisons. This implementation executor did
not inspect the original reference: its supported transfers failed. The accepted
comparison image remains Library version 1, with no pixel changes afterward.

Validation includes full debug/release JVM suites, Compose/Robolectric checks,
exact protected inner-face pixels at 240/360/720/1000px for all three battery bands
and unknown, original fastener seats and unchanged touch targets, boundary reading
and View/wallpaper lifecycle tests, explicit lint, unsigned R8 assembly,
instrumentation-source compilation, 24 release safeguards and unsigned APK
provenance/alignment inspection. Historical artwork revisions retain hash-checked
fixtures rather than weakening their old golden assertions. Render evidence is
Robolectric/API35/native Skia, not Android device screenshots or phone performance.
No new device instrumentation, physical TalkBack/OEM-host or frame-time profiling
was performed. Private assets, production certificate pin, independent v2
verification and both protected release approvals remain unchanged.

Version code: 19. Package: `ai.nanosearch.launcher`.
